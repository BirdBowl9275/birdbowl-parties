Section 1

Signing in

There is no password. The app emails you a one-time code each time you sign in.

  1. 1
    Open the app and enter your work email.
  2. 2
    Check your email for a one-time code, and type it in.
  3. 3
    You're in. New staff are taken to Availability first if next week is not filled in yet.
Waiting for approval?When you first sign in, a manager must approve your account. Until then you will see a waiting screen. This is normal. Ask your manager if it takes too long.

Section 2

Entering your availability

Tell us the times you can work next week. Your manager uses this to build the schedule.

  1. 1
    Open Availability from the menu.
  2. 2
    Tick each day you can work.
  3. 3
    Set the From and Until times for that day, like 5:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m.
  4. 4
    Press Save availability.
Locked weeksThe page shows a cutoff time in Eastern time. After it passes, the week is locked and you can no longer change it. Ask a manager to change it for you.
Can't work any day next week?Untick every day and press Save. That still counts as your answer, so the app stops asking you to fill it in.

Your schedule

This is your home screen. It shows your party time slots for the next two weeks.

Details hidden

The details appear at 8:00 a.m. on the day of the party, or sooner if a manager locks the assignment.

Details shown

You can see the customer name, the number of kids, the party type and any notes.

Section 4

Releasing a party

Can't make a party? You can release it yourself, as long as it is still early enough.

  1. 1
    Find the party on Your schedule.
  2. 2
    Press Release this party.
  3. 3
    The app looks for another eligible hostess who is free. If one is found, the party moves to her. If not, the party goes back to your manager to cover.
After 48 hours, the button goes away.A party cannot be released in the last 48 hours before it starts. A party your manager has locked cannot be released either. If you can't attend, tell your manager straight away.

Party Hostess Scheduler5 · How parties are shared out
Section 5

How parties are shared out

The app suggests who gets each party, so the work is spread fairly. Your manager reviews and can always change a suggestion.

  1. 1
    Only people who are free can be offered a party. You must be free for the whole time from arrival to the end, with at least 30 minutes between parties. You can't be on a front-desk or server shift at the same time.
  2. 2
    Big parties go out first, the ones with the most kids.
  3. 3
    Party hostesses come first, and other roles only if no hostess is free.
  4. 4
    Hours decide the order. Each person can work up to 38 hours a week, counting shifts and parties. Anyone under 32 hours is offered parties before anyone at 32 or more. Among them, the fewest hours come first.
  5. 5
    If hours are level, the app checks that a party hostess's average kids per party stays within 20% of the team average. Then it looks at the total kids you already have this week, so the fewest kids come first. If it is still level, the person who was free on more days in the last 30 days goes first.
Being unavailable doesn't count against you.The fairness count looks only at the days you were free, so a week away doesn't put you behind.
If something looks wrongAsk your manager to review a party you were offered, or your hours. The app's suggestion isn't final until your manager saves it.

Section 6

Signing out, and common questions

Sign out when you finish, especially on a shared device.

  1. 1
    Scroll to the bottom of Your schedule and press Sign out.

Common questions

I didn't get the code.
Check your spam folder. Then go back to the sign-in screen and ask for a new code.

Why can't I change my availability?
The week locks after the cutoff. Ask a manager to change it.

Why can't I see the party details?
Details appear at 8:00 a.m. on the day of the party, or sooner if a manager has locked the assignment.

The app keeps sending me to Availability.
Save your availability for next week, even if you can't work any day. After that you go straight to your schedule.

I can't release a party.
The release button closes 48 hours before the party starts. Contact your manager.

Need more help?Ask your manager. For a sign-in problem, ask them to check that your account is approved.
