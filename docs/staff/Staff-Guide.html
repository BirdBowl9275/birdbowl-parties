1Signing in

There is no password. The app emails you a one-time code each time you sign in.

  1. a Open the app and enter your work email.
  2. b Check your email for a one-time code and type it in.
  3. c You are in. New staff go to Availability first if next week is not filled in yet.
Waiting for approval? When you first sign in, a manager must approve your account. Until then you will see a waiting screen. This is normal. Ask your manager if it takes too long.

2Entering your availability

Tell us the times you can work next week. Your manager uses this to build the schedule.

  1. 1 Open Availability from the menu.
  2. 2 Tick each day you can work.
  3. 3 Set the From and Until times for that day.
  4. 4 Press Save availability.
Locked week. The page shows a cutoff time, in Eastern time. After it passes, the week is locked. You can no longer change it. Ask a manager to change it for you.
Can't work any day next week? Untick every day and press Save. This still counts as your answer, so the app stops asking you to fill it in.

3Your schedule

This is your home screen. It shows your party time slots for the next two weeks.

  • • Each card is one party time slot, with the day and start and end times.
  • • Details hidden means the party details are not shown yet. They appear at 8:00 a.m. on the day of the party, or sooner if a manager locks the assignment.
  • • Details shown means you can see the customer name, number of kids, party type and any notes.
  • • A line that says Party starts means you arrive earlier than the party's start time.

4Releasing a party

Can't make a party? You can release it yourself, if it is still early enough.

  1. 1 Find the party on Your schedule.
  2. 2 Press Release this party.
  3. 3 The app looks for another eligible hostess who is free. If one is found, the party moves to her. If not, the party goes back to your manager to cover.
The button disappears 48 hours before the party. From then on, the party cannot be released. Also, a party that your manager has locked cannot be released. Call or message your manager straight away if you cannot attend.

5How parties are shared out

The app suggests who gets each party, so that the work is spread fairly. Your manager can always change a suggestion.

  1. 1 Only people who are free can be offered a party. You must be available for the whole time, from arrival to the end. You need a gap of at least 30 minutes between parties. You can't be on a front-desk or server shift at the same time.
  2. 2 Big parties are handed out first (the most kids first).
  3. 3 Party hostesses come first, then the other roles, if no hostess is free.
  4. 4 Hours decide the order. Each person can work up to 38 hours a week, counting shifts and parties. Anyone under 32 hours is offered parties before anyone at 32 or more. Among them, the fewest hours come first.
  5. 5 If hours are level, the app looks at the number of parties you have this week, then the average number of kids you have had. Then it looks at how often you were available in the last 30 days. The last tie-break is alphabetical.
Being unavailable doesn't count against you. The fairness count looks only at the days you were free, so a week away doesn't put you behind.
Ask your manager if you want a party you have been offered reviewed, or if your hours look wrong. The app's suggestion is not final until your manager saves it.

6Signing out, and common questions

Sign out when you finish, especially on a shared device.

  • • Scroll to the bottom of Your schedule and press Sign out.

Common questions

I didn't get the code. Check your spam folder. Then go back to the sign-in screen and ask for a new code.

Why can't I change my availability? The week is locked after the cutoff. Ask a manager to change it.

Why can't I see the party details? Details appear at 8:00 a.m. on the day of the party, or sooner if a manager has locked the assignment.

The app keeps sending me to Availability. Save your availability for next week, even if you can't work any day. Once saved, you will go straight to your schedule.

I can't release a party. The release button closes 48 hours before the party starts. Contact your manager.

Need more help? Ask your manager. For a sign-in problem, ask them to check that your account is approved.
