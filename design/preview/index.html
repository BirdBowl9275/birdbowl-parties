Bird Bowl Parties: design direction

Calm neutrals, one brand red for the logo and primary actions, a separate brick red for errors, a blue focus ring. Light and dark are equal; the page follows your system until you pick a theme above. All names are placeholders.

The red circle marked "BB" is a stand-in for the real Bird Bowl logo asset. Full spec: design/design-system.md.
